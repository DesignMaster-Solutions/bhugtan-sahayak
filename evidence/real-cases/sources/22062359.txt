First Appellate Authority order : Not on record Complaint Filed on : 31/10/2020 Date of Hearing : 20/04/2022 Date of Decision : 20/04/2022 The following were present: Complainant: Present over VC Respondent: Swarup Dutta, CPIO, present over intra VC Information Sought:

The Complainant has stated that registration of his grandmother's name Rampati W/o Late Ram Autar for PM Kisan Samman Nidhi has been made by U.P. Agriculture Deptt. on 31 July, 2019 on official website - upagriculture.com with Kisan ID: 867580427703118 but till today not a single installment has been credited into her account number: 33800088028 (SBI) In connection with the same he has sought the following information:
1. What is the status of PFMS response of her?
2. As to when the data was received by NIC Lucknow from the concerned Department?
3. As to why not a single instalment has been credited to her account till today?

Grounds for Complaint The CPIO did not provide the desired information. Submissions made by Complainant and Respondent during Hearing:

The complainant submitted that the reply of the CPIO was not proper as rather than transferring the RTI application to the concerned holder of the information, it was merely stated that the information is maintained by Agricultural department of the State Government.
The CPIO submitted that an appropriate reply was given to the complainant on 29.10.2020. He also reiterated the contents of his written submissions dated 13.04.2022.

Observations:

From a perusal of the relevant case records, it is noted that the CPIO in his reply had stated that application and data is maintained by the Agricultural department and NIC has no role to play on the application. The complainant is aggrieved as according to him if the information was available with the Agricultural Department, the CPIO was to transfer the RTI application u/s 6(3) of the RTI Act. However, the CPIO in his written submissions while elaborating on the reply given by him has now stated that the information is maintained by the Agricultural Department of the State Government. The Commission is unable to find any flaw in the reply as Section 6(3) of the RTI Act is applicable only in such cases where the CPIO knows that the information is held by another public authority and not in such cases where the information is held by any department of State Government. Further, the queries raised by the complainant were all hypothetical in nature, not covered u/s 2(f) of the RTI Act and on that ground also, no relief could have been given to the complainant. However, the CPIO is cautioned to remain careful in future and to ensure that a complete reply is given to all the applicants.
Decision:
In view of the above, since the complaint is not established, no action lies.
The complaint is disposed of accordingly.
