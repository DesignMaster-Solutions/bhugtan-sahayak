Information sought

and background of the case:

The Appellant filed an RTI application dated 24.04.2018 seeking information on the following six points in connection with her widow pension application dated 22.12.2017:
1. Provide action taken report/record on my aforesaid application for widow pension
2. Please inform, if my, aforesaid application were accepted or rejected.
3. If my application rejected, provide reasons for the same with the copy of action taken report/record. And etc. The PIO, D.O (South) Delhi furnished a point wise reply to the appellant vide letter dated 27.04.2018.

Dissatisfied with the reply received from the PIO, the Appellant filed a First Appeal dated 11.06.2018 whereupon the FAA observed that the information has been furnished and the FAA vide order dated 28.06.2018 upheld the reply of PIO and disposed of his first appeal.

Feeling aggrieved and dissatisfied, the Appellant approached the Commission in the instant Second Appeal.

Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:

A written submission has been received from PIO & District Officer (South) vide letter dated 11.06.2020 wherein she has stated that the widow-pension of Mrs. Geeta is being regularly credited in her bank account no. 9125xxxxxx9807, Syndicate Bank, Sheikh Sari Branch, New Delhi.
In order to ensure social distancing and prevent the spread of the pandemic, COVID-19, audio hearings were scheduled after giving prior notice to both the parties.
The Appellant participated in the hearing on being contacted on her telephone. She stated that she is receiving the widow pension from the past one year.
Respondent is represented by Smt. Anita Gaur, PIO/DO-South and Shri Subhash Chandra Agarwal, Rep. of PIO through audio conference. Smt. Anita Gaur, PIO/DO-South submitted that the Appellant's widow pension was stopped since her Aadhaar number was not linked, however, the moment Appellant's aadhaar number got linked, widow pension in question is being credited to her Syndicate Bank account.
Decision In view of the proceedings during hearing, Commission finds no further scope of intervention in the instant matter.
