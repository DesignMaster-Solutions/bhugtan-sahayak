1. Petitioner has filed this petition seeking following relief (s) :-

Neutral Citation 2024:CGHC:26030 "10.1 That, this Hon'ble Court may kindly be pleased to direct the respondent authorities to consider the case of petitioner for grant of remaining even ahead installments under PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Scheme in favor of the petitioner.
10.2 That, this Hon'ble Court may kindly be pleased to direct the respondents authority to grant the benefit under the scheme of Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) of remaining installments 6th to 16th to the petitioner which is not paid, whereas he is a eligible beneficiary, in the interest of justice. 10.3 That, this Hon'ble Court may kindly be pleased to grant any other relief/relief's in favor of the petitioner, which the Hon'ble Court deemed fit & just in the facts and circumstances of the case, including awarding of the costs to the petitioner."

2. Learned counsel for petitioner submits that petitioner is entitled for Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) installments. Initially he was paid installment of Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi No.1 to 5, thereafter, the installments towards Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi was not being deposited in the account of petitioner. As per his information as of now 17 installments have already been deposited in the account of eligible persons and petitioner is not being paid the said amount since six installments till date. Petitioner approached the office of Senior Agriculture Development Officer, Block - Sakti, District - Sakti and considering the claim of petitioner and his entitlement, respondent No.6 wrote letter on 13.09.2023 to respondent No.8 mentioning that in the portal in front of the name of petitioner is showing stop payment and therefore, it be accordingly corrected and it be released. The letter was written in Neutral Citation 2024:CGHC:26030 the month September, 2023, however, even after lapse of about 10 months, no steps have been taken by respondent No.8 nor petitioner has been intimated about any obstruction in removing the entry of 'stop payment' in front of name of petitioner on the concerned portal. He submits that as the respondent No.8 is sitting over the claim of petitioner and therefore, respondent No.8 be directed to consider the claim of petitioner and to take necessary steps pursuant to the letter dated 13.09.2023 of respondent No.6.

3. Learned counsel for State would submit that grievance of petitioner is with regard to non-payment of Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi according to the policy formulated by Central Government for that redressal mechanism is also formulated. He however submits that petitioner has approached respondent No.6 raising his grievance that amount of Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi is not being deposited in his account. Respondent No.6 has already forwarded a letter to respondent No.8 for taking necessary steps considering the grievance of petitioner. If for any reason the steps could not have been taken it will be taken in accordance with law.

4. Counsel for respondent No.1 would submit that grievance of petitioner is against respondents No. 8.

5. I have heard learned counsel for parties and perused the documents placed on record.

Neutral Citation 2024:CGHC:26030

6. Grievance raised by petitioner that he is entitled for the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi, his name is registered in the portal, of which he also received five installment of the same and since sixth installments the amount is not being deposited in his account and upon verification it revealed to the petitioner that in front of his name in the portal it is mentioned as stop payment. No reason has been assigned to him thereafter. Further taking note of the fact that respondent no.6 has already written a letter to respondent No.8 for taking necessary steps for redressal of the grievance of petitioner as early as on 13.09.2023, I find it appropriate to dispose of this petition at this stage directing respondent No.8 to take decision on the claim of petitioner pursuant to the letter dated 13.09.2023 in accordance with law expeditiously preferably within a period of four weeks from the date of receipt of the order and petitioner be intimated with the decision taken by him. Learned State counsel is also directed to send information to respondent No.8 about the order passed by this Court.

7. Accordingly, this petition is disposed of with the aforesaid observations and directions.
