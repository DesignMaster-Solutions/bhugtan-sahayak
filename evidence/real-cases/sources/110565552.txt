 HON'BLE ROHIT RANJAN AGARWAL, J.      

1. The writ Court on 20.9.2023 while disposing of Writ-C No. 31192 of 2023 had passed the following order;

"1. Heard Shri Vinod Kumar Pandey, learned counsel for the petitioner and learned Standing Counsel appearing for the State-respondents.
2. Solitary grievance of the petitioner appears to be that beyond the first installment, the remaining installments under the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi are not being released to the petitioner.
3. Learned Standing Counsel has referred to certain discrepancies having arisen with respect to AADHAR Number of the petitioner as originally disclosed and as came to be disclosed by the petitioner, later.
4. Arising from such circumstances, the petitioner's representation dated 5.6.2023 appears to be pending before respondent No.2.
5. In view of above, no useful purpose will be served in keeping the petition pending or calling for the counter affidavit, at this stage.
6. Without entering into the merits, the writ petition is disposed of with a direction upon respondent No.2-UP-Krishi Nideshak, Aligarh, to grant a short opportunity of hearing to the petition and pass a reasoned and speaking order on the petitioner's representation dated 5.6.2023 as expeditiously as possible, preferably, within a period of one month from the date, petitioner files a copy of this order before the authority concerned.
7. It is further observed that if the claim of the petitioner is found to be correct, the remaining installments would be released in favour of the petitioner without any further delay."

2. In compliance affidavit filed by the State it has been stated that as the form filled up by the applicant was not complete, he was required to submit fresh form for release of amount under Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojna and representation was decided on 1.7.2024, which has been brought on record as annexure No. 1 to the compliance affidavit.

3. As opposite party had required the applicant to move a fresh application and get his claim registered on web-portal for getting the benefit under Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojna, the representation stood decided.

4. Learned counsel for applicant has further submitted that the writ Court had observed that in case claim of applicant is found to be correct, remaining installments would be released in his favour.

5. From reading of the representation decided by the opposite party on 1.7.2024 it is clear that as correct AADHAR Card number was not provided by the applicant, his application was not processed and he was required to upload the correct form with valid AADHAR number, which he had failed to do so.

6. As the order of writ Court has been complied by the opposite party by deciding the representation of the applicant, contempt application has rendered infructuous and same stands dismissed. Contempt notice stands discharged.
