Information sought

:

The Appellant filed an RTI application dated 13/12/2021 seeking the following information:
"My Kisan Samman Nidhi has not been credited in my bank account since August 2020 in relation to this I have many time visited to Agriculture Department office at charthawal and Muzaffarnagar but they were not give me a appropriate answer to me after that I have registered a grievance on pgportal in relation to these the following information a required 1 after the three month of registered a complaint on pgportal DOAAC/E/2021/14758 what are you an action or it? 2 why I was not getting my Kisan Samman Nidhi ? 3 By whom default I was not getting my Kisan Samman Nidhi and who is responsible for that?
4 in how many days you will start my Kisan Samman Nidhi?"

The CPIO furnished a reply to the appellant on 10/01/2022 stating as under:

"I am to refer to your request for Information under RTI Act, 2005, received vide letter dated 13/12/2021 and to say that as per the information available on the web portal www.pmkisan.gov.in, you have not got benefits after payment of 61st instalment in the month of August, 2020 since the data of beneficiary has been rejected by PFMS and the ground for rejection is that the no response received from banks within specified days. You are, therefore, advised to approach the bank branch for ascertaining the reason of non-response. Further, in case the bank account is not operative/active, you may change the account number or mode of payment from account to aadhar with the assistance of nodal officer of PM-Kisan Scheme in your district/block. The benefits would be provided as and when verified data is re-processed by the concerned state authorities."

Being dissatisfied, the appellant filed a First Appeal dated 19/01/2022. FAA's order, dated 25/01/2022, upheld the reply of CPIO.

Feeling aggrieved and dissatisfied, the appellant approached the Commission with the instant Second Appeal on the ground of alleged incorrect and misleading response from the CPIO.

Relevant Facts emerging during Hearing:

The following were present:-
Appellant: Not present.
Respondent: Dhrub Kumar, U.S. (Farmers Welfare) & CPIO present in person.
The CPIO submitted that the genesis of instant matter relates to Appellant's grievance for non-disbursement of benefits under ' Kisan Samman Nidhi' after a particular period for which he made the averred complaint on PG portal and later, filed the instant RTI Application. He further submitted that the reply on PG portal to his complaint has already been provided on 18.10.2021 detailing the reason for rejection of his claim owing to non-seeding of agricultural land of the Appellant with the claim ; as also a detailed reply has been furnished to the him as mentioned above.
Decision:
The Commission upon a perusal of records observes that the earlier reply of the CPIO was not categorical as was discussed/stated by him during hearing. While the Commission finds no reason to intervene into the merits of the availability of records as stated by the CPIO, but it was incumbent upon the CPIO to have informed the Appellant in writing in the manner it was explained by him during the hearing.
In view of the foregoing, the Commission directs the CPIO to provide a revised categorical reply by incorporating the entire facts as mentioned by him during hearing. The said reply/ information should be provided by the CPIO free of cost to the Appellant within 2 days from the date of receipt of this order under due intimation to the Commission.
The appeal is disposed of accordingly.
