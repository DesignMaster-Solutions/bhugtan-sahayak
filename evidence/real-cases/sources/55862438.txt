Information sought

and background of the case:

Appellant filed RTI application dated 15.11.2018seeking information on four points:-
1. Supply the information regarding widow pension of the applicant.
2. Supply the information how the applicant came to now that her aadhaar card is linked with widow pension department.
3. Supply the information how much time will take for releasing the widow pension to the applicant.
4. Supply the information whether any other information is required from the applicant about releasing the widow pension.

PIO/O/o. the District Officer (NW-I), Dept., of Women & Child Development vide letter dated 10.12.2018 provided point wise information to the Appellant. Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed the First Appeal dated 27.12.2017. Feeling aggrieved with no response received from the FAA, the Appellant approached the Commission with the instant Second Appeal.

Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:

Appellant submits that she started receiving pensionary benefits 2011 onwards which continued till 23rd March 2015. When she didn't receive any pension for

2 months after 23rd March 2015, she inquired and found out that her pension was being credited in two bank accounts i.e. IDBI Account and Adhaar linked SBI account. Appellant claims to be unaware of the situation and declares to have returned the additional amount of Rs. 9000/- through demand draft in favour of the public authority. Appellant is aggrieved that though her pension was resumed from January 2019 onwards, she has not received any amount that is accrued for the months before that, since March 2015, for no fault of hers.

Respondent had filed a submission dated 16.09.2019 which indicates as per the available records the facts and circumstances leading to the present appeal. Reiterating the contents of the submission thereof, respondent maintains that there is no provision to provide arrears for the intervening period and as per the department policy, amount pertaining to only 6 months is provided in such cases to the pensioners.

Decision:

The Appellant is a widow and her case appears to be a grievance. Perusal of the submission filed by the Respondent indicate that it has been incorrectly stated in the submission dated 16.09.2019 that the Appellant has not filed the First Appeal. However, perusal of the available records reveal that the First Appeal was duly filed by the Appellant on 27.12.2018. Commission observes that neither FAA hearing was conducted nor an order was passed by the competent authority.
Though Commission is not a grievance redressal forum, in taking a lenient view in the interest of justice and social welfare, Commission deems it fit that the case be remanded to the FAA/Sh. S.C. Vats, Jt. Director, Dept. of Women & Child Development (Govt. of NCT of Delhi)to adjudicate over the matter and decide the same on merits giving specific directions to the PIO to provide reasonable assistance to the Appellant along with the deficient information, if any, under the special circumstances of the Appellant. The appeal shall be decided by a reasoned, speaking order, under intimation to the Commission, within 1 month from the date of this order, failing which appropriate action for non-compliance shall commence suo motu.
The appeal is disposed off accordingly, with the above directions.
