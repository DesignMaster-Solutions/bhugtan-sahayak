Hon'ble Surya Prakash Kesarwani,J.
 

Hon'ble Anish Kumar Gupta,J.

Heard Sri Rajesh Kumar Shukla, learned counsel for the petitioner and Sri Rajeev Gupta, learned Additional Chief Standing counsel for the State-respondents.

On 28.02.2023, this court passed the following order:

"In response to the order dated 08.02.2023, Ms. Uttara Bahuguna, learned Additional Chief Standing Counsel has place detailed instruction dated 27.02.2023 sent by the Deputy Agriculture Director appending therein the beneficial status. She submits that so far as the Aadhaar Demo Authentication Status is concerned the same is shown as "Rejected, eKYC Done : No".

In this background, she submits that on the portal petitioner has not filed the correct e-KYC and petitioner has also not done land record assessment and verification from tehsil, therefore, the second installment could not be credited in the account of the petitioner.

On the request of learned counsel for the petitioner, the matter is adjourned.

Put up this matter as fresh on 03.03.2023."

Learned Additional Chief Standing counsel states on instructions of the respondent No.4 dated 01.03.2023 that nine installments were inadvertently paid in another account due to wrong feeding of account number and now correction has been made and subsequent installments, i.e. 10th and onward installments shall be paid in the account of the petitioner through Adhar number.

In view of the aforesaid, we dispose of this writ petition with the direction to the respondent No.4 to ensure that nine installments under the 'Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojna' is paid to the petitioner within one month from the date of submission of certified copy of this order, if not paid so far. He shall also ensure that subsequent installments are also paid to the petitioner as per scheme.

Order Date :- 17.3.2023 NLY
